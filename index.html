Good to know

Why do some styles look different after pasting?

The styles use Unicode characters. Their appearance depends on the font and app that display them. Some characters may appear plain or as missing glyphs.

Why does typing stay plain in some fields?

Live typing requires IBus on Linux and a compatible text field. Vellire keeps password, private and terminal fields plain. In the app, choose Use Normal to return to standard text.

Do I need an account?

You can preview text and use the local typing features without signing in. Google sign-in is optional.

How do I uninstall?

On Windows, remove Vellire under Settings → Apps. On Debian-based Linux, run sudo apt remove vellire.
